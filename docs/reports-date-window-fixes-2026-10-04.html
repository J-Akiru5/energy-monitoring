Reports PH Date-Window Fixes

Four defects around report dates are fixed. Reports now treats every date as a Philippine (UTC+8) calendar date — the month window no longer silently drops the first eight PH hours of the 1st (6.186 of 46.682 kWh on the test fixture). The average/day divisor now uses the PH days actually inside the selected range (4.24 → 5.30 on the fixture), the PDF no longer calls a rolling 7-day value a "calendar week", and History now shows the exact PH window its totals cover — with a hint when a week straddles two months. History's window definitions themselves are unchanged.

The student's observation — not a bug

A WVSU student saw History show Week 73.833 kWh > Month 52.312 kWh for 10/01/2026 and flagged it. It was investigated and is correct behavior: History's week is the PH calendar week Mon–Sun (Sep 28 – Oct 4), which includes three September days, while the month is Oct 1–31. Different windows, different totals — no code change to History's window definitions was made, per scope.

What the investigation did find were four real defects on the Reports side, fixed in this branch.

1 · Reports date handling ran on UTC, not PH

fixed

Effect: 00:00–08:00 PH on the 1st was never loaded

The API's current_month preset started at Date.UTC(y, m, 1) — 08:00 PH on the 1st — and today used UTC day bounds; the page's custom ranges were built as ${date}T00:00:00.000Z/23:59:59.999Z. Every date input was therefore shifted 8 hours against the PH calendar.

Fixture (PH Oct 1 00:00 → Oct 4 17:00, monotonic counter): month-to-date was 40.496 kWh before, 46.682 kWh after — the first 8 PH hours on the 1st (6.186 kWh) are no longer dropped. New helpers live in apps/web/src/lib/phTime.ts; parseReportFilters() gained an optional injectable now for deterministic tests.

2 · Average/day divided by the wrong number of days

avg/day was month-to-date within the selected range ÷ the full PH day-of-month. A range starting on the 3rd divided 8 days of energy by 10 — understating the daily rate. Three options were put to Jeff with measured numbers:

decision recorded

Option B — approved by Jeff, 2026-10-04

avg/day = month-to-date within the selected range ÷ PH calendar days from max(range start, month start) to the latest reading, inclusive (min 1); week = day × 7; month = day × 30. No extra fetching, and the averages stay consistent with the filter — matching the tile's "Auto-updates with filter changes". The tile note now reads "month-to-date ÷ days in range". Default range behavior is unchanged (range start = month start).

3 · PDF week label

The kWh report printed "Week (calendar week)" for a rolling last-7-days value. Wording only — the calculation is untouched:

- Week (calendar week): 40.2260 kWh  |  PHP 402.26
+ Week (last 7 days):    40.2260 kWh  |  PHP 402.26

A Monday-start calendar week was raised as a possible alternative and deliberately not implemented, per scope.

4 · History now shows its window

The History summary now displays the exact PH window the totals cover, using the API's existing rangeStart/rangeEnd (end adjusted inclusive by 1 ms). For a week that crosses a month boundary, a one-line hint names the earlier month:

Window: Mon Sep 28 – Sun Oct 4, 2026 · includes days from September

This is exactly the explanation for the student's week > month question, now visible in the product. No totals or window definitions changed.
