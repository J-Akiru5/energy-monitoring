# Energy Monitoring Unit — On-Site Check (Plain-English Guide)

Hello! This guide asks you to do three simple checks on the energy monitoring unit (called **ESP32-CICT-001**) at your site. It takes about 15 minutes. You will **not** open or touch anything electrical — everything is done from a website and by watching a small appliance.

## Please read first — safety

- Do **NOT** open the device box or touch any wires.
- Everything here is done from the website Jeff gives you.
- If you see smoke, smell burning, or hear unusual crackling: **stop, keep away, and contact Jeff**.
- If the unit is inside a locked electrical panel, do not force access — ask facilities/electrician.

## What you need

- A phone or computer with internet.
- The website address and login from Jeff.
- A small appliance connected to the unit that is OK to switch off for a few seconds (for example a lamp) — please confirm with whoever uses it first.
- About 15 minutes.

## Test 1 — Is the unit online?

1. Log in to the website.
2. Find the device named **ESP32-CICT-001**.
3. It should show **online**, and the voltage/power numbers should **change every few seconds**.
4. If it says offline or the numbers never change, write that down and go to "What to send back".

## Test 2 — Does the power switch work?

1. Open the control page for **ESP32-CICT-001** (the page with Trip / Reset buttons).
2. Press **Trip** (turn off). Watch the appliance: within a few seconds it should **turn OFF**. You may hear a small click from the box — that's a good sign.
3. Press **Reset** (turn on). The appliance should **turn back ON** within a few seconds.
4. Do steps 2–3 one more time, so you've seen it twice.
5. If it doesn't work, do not keep pressing. Write down exactly what happened.

## Test 3 — Photos

- One photo of the device box (**closed — do not open it**).
- One photo of the appliance.
- One photo of the website screen after the test.

## What happens next

Jeff will review your answers and photos. If the automatic safety test is needed, it will be scheduled separately with a **licensed electrician** — you do not need to do anything for that, and please do not attempt it yourself.

Thank you!
